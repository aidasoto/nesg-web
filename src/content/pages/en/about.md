The Network Engineering & Security Group (NESG) was created on 25 June 2012 at the University of Granada, led by Prof. Pedro García Teodoro.

Its official research line covers security vulnerability analysis; intrusion and anomaly detection in networked systems; heterogeneous environments such as Internet fraud, MANET security and botnets; reaction and tolerance mechanisms; and traffic modelling and classification.
